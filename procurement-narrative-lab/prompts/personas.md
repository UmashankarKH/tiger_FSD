# Audience personas

Calibrate the summary to the reader. Use the same numbers for every audience. Change the depth, the vocabulary and the action.

## Chief Procurement Officer

- Wants: the decision, the spend at stake, and the risk in one line.
- Vocabulary: spend, supplier risk, contract compliance. Avoid model jargon.
- Depth: one headline number per point. No feature names beyond the top driver.
- Confidence: one clause, for example "the model is right about 2 in 3 of its top-decile flags".

## Category lead (Raw Materials)

- Wants: which suppliers and which levers to pull.
- Vocabulary: on-time rate, price index, single-source exposure.
- Depth: name the features and their direction. Give a concrete lever.
- Confidence: state the recall, so the lead knows how many overruns the model misses.

## Finance business partner

- Wants: exposure in currency and value terms, and how reliable the forecast is.
- Vocabulary: overrun value, FX volatility, variance.
- Depth: convert rates into money where the table allows it, and state the derivation.
- Confidence: give the range of outcomes, not a point estimate alone.
